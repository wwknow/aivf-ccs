# Contributor License Agreement (CLA) — Template

**Status: template for project adoption before accepting external contributions.**

This CLA is intended to preserve the project's ability to maintain an
the Project's long-term licensing flexibility while keeping contributor rights explicit.

Before enabling external pull requests, replace **Project Steward** below with
the actual legal person or entity that will hold and administer these rights,
and have this agreement reviewed for the jurisdictions in which contributors
will participate.

## 1. Definitions

“Contribution” means any original work of authorship submitted by You for
inclusion in the Project.

“Project” means the AIVF CCS source project and its official repositories.

“Project Steward” means the legal person or entity designated by the Project to
receive the licenses granted by this agreement.

## 2. Copyright license

You retain ownership of Your Contribution.

You grant the Project Steward and recipients of software distributed by the
Project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable
copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display,
publicly perform, sublicense, and distribute Your Contribution and derivative
works of it.

For clarity, this grant permits the Project Steward to include Your
Contribution in:

- official Project distributions.

## 3. Patent license

You grant the Project Steward and recipients of software distributed by the
Project a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable patent
license to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise
transfer Your Contribution, where such license applies only to patent claims
licensable by You that are necessarily infringed by Your Contribution alone or
by its combination with the Project to which it was submitted.

## 4. Authority

You represent that You are legally entitled to grant the licenses above.

If Your employer or another party has rights in Your Contribution, You
represent that You have received permission to submit it under this CLA.

## 5. Originality

You represent that each Contribution is Your original creation or that You have
sufficient rights to submit it under these terms. You will identify material
third-party code included in a Contribution.

## 6. No support obligation

You are not expected to provide support for Your Contribution unless separately
agreed.

## 7. How acceptance is recorded

The Project may record acceptance electronically, for example through a CLA bot,
signed web form, signed document, or verified GitHub account workflow.
